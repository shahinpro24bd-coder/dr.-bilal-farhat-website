About Dr. Bilal Farhat Ali

Who is Dr. Bilal Farhat Ali?

Dr. Bilal Farhat Ali earned his MBBS from Akhtar Saeed Medical & Dental College in Lahore, Pakistan, and completed internships at Jinnah Hospital, Farooq Hospital and Akhtar Saeed Trust Hospital. After U.S. clinical experience at Houston Methodist West Hospital and a post-doctoral research fellowship at Mayo Clinic, he now practices Internal Medicine in Cincinnati, Ohio.

His training spans busy teaching hospitals in Lahore, clinical rotations across the United States and research at Mayo Clinic. “Good medicine starts with listening. I want every patient to leave understanding their condition, their numbers and their plan — in the language they are most comfortable with.”
